The man in
the yellow tux.

Jesse Cole is the founder of Fans First Entertainment and the owner of the Banana Ball Championship League, including the Savannah Bananas, the Party Animals, the Firefighters, the Texas Tailgaters, the Loco Beach Coconuts, and the Indianapolis Clowns.

The origin

He didn’t set out
to be a speaker.

He set out to make baseball fun.

His site tells the story plainly: Fans First Entertainment owns the Savannah Bananas, and the mission is to Make Baseball Fun by creating the Greatest Show in Sports.

The team has sold out every game since its inaugural season in Savannah and has grown a ticket waitlist of millions of fans from all over the world.

Inc. reported that he sold his house and emptied his savings to buy the team. The bet was on the experience, not the product.

Today he is an international keynote speaker, the inventor of Banana Ball, and the author of three books.
