Who is Jesse Cole?

Jesse Cole is the founder of Fans First Entertainment and the owner of the Banana Ball Championship League, including the Savannah Bananas. He is the author of Find Your Yellow Tux, Fans First, and Banana Ball, and an international keynote speaker.

What does Jesse speak about?

Two signature keynotes: The Fans First Way, on the 5 E's to create raving fans, and How to Design the Ultimate Customer Experience. All keynotes are customized for your event.

How do we book Jesse?

Speaking inquiries are handled by Li Hayes at sid@goleeward.com or 203-314-2441. You can also reach Jesse at jesse@findyouryellowtux.com or 912-712-2482.

Where is he based?

Savannah, Georgia. The office address published on his site is 2934 Bee Road, Savannah, GA 31404.

What is Banana Ball?

Banana Ball is the version of baseball Jesse invented: a two-hour time limit, no walks, no bunting, no stepping out of the box, no mound visits, and fans can catch a foul ball for an out.

What is the yellow tux?

It is the image behind his first book, Find Your Yellow Tux, and a shorthand for the idea he speaks about: standing out instead of blending in.
